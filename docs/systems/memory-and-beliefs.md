# Memory & Beliefs

Everything a being knows, and when it learned it.

## Decided

- One structure, two uses. A memory is something that happened to it; a
  belief is something it knows about a place or an object. Both are a
  timestamped record of what this being knows.
- Beings act on beliefs. Nothing asks the world for the nearest food — a
  being goes to the place it knows about, which may be gone.
- Strength is computed from age on read, never ticked down.
- Capped per being: keep the strongest handful, drop the rest.
- Spreads only when beings actually meet. An event pushes to whoever was
  present; nothing scans everyone against everyone.
- Personality is a fixed base plus the pull of currently-strong
  memories, computed the same way.
- A memory list doubles as the being's readable history.

## Known territory

- What a settlement's beings collectively know about the wider world: an
  entry per thing, carrying where it is, what is there, and when it was
  last seen. Animals, forest, other settlements, water, farmland,
  mountains.
- The player is never gated by this. They see the whole world regardless.
- An entry should update its last-seen mark every time a being actually
  re-observes it, rather than freezing the first sighting as permanently
  true.
- **Things stop being there because something physically happened** —
  eaten, moved on — not because a decay probability was rolled.

## Two kinds of place, deliberately not the same

- Known territory is about the wider world a settlement knows *about*.
- A store, a house or a farm is a place *at* the settlement.
- A water source could plausibly matter to both. Whether that is one
  thing referenced twice or two related things is not decided.

## Expeditions

A being leaves the settlement and either returns, adding what it found,
or does not — and not returning is meant to convey something too. Not
designed further.

## Open

- The naming for the two kinds of place.
- What a being failing to return actually conveys.
