# Settlement & Jobs

The settlement as a real thing, and the coordinator that hands out work.

## Decided

- A settlement owns its members, its stores, and its buildings. Its
  shortages create the available work.
- **Anything with a semi-fixed location and a community is a settlement**
  — a human village, an animal warren, a grove of tree folk. One class,
  and each species carries its own name for what its settlement is
  called.
- The job manager is its own module, holding the universal jobs every
  being does wherever it lives — eat, sleep — and taking settlement
  information as input.
- Beings hold their own references. A villager knows its home; a house
  knows its occupants.
- Coordination lives here so beings never negotiate with each other.
  Anything needing two beings to agree goes through the settlement.
- Work is offered, never assigned. A being asks and chooses.
- A lone being still needs tasking, and gets its own pool of one.

## Buildings

- Beings decide to build. There is no player build menu or placement UI.
- Shelter is not housing. Shelter is the baseline survival need and can be
  satisfied by something as minimal as a nearby tree. Housing is a
  constructed upgrade, and is not required to survive.
- A house needs a real position, and a placeholder model is enough.
- Building needs wood, and needs a real reason — a population outgrowing
  its housing — not a flat one-house-per-N rule.

## The store

Two tiers, not three: a marked spot on the ground, which later becomes a
built store. Beings walk to it to deposit and collect, so **it needs a
visible marker from the start** — otherwise nothing on screen explains
where everyone is walking.

## Wood comes from trees

Trees are a real, harvestable resource rather than a number that starts at
fifty. That means trees have to come back: either they seed themselves, or
beings plant them, and planting them is the more interesting answer
because it is visible work that makes the world feel tended.

Tree folk are part of this rather than separate from it.

## Farm

- A cycle: seed, grow, harvest, re-seed. Seeding is a real act — a plot
  sits idle until a being walks over and plants it.
- **Seeding happens whenever a field is below capacity, not only when it
  is empty.** Continuous topping-up gives steady produce instead of the
  whole field ripening and emptying in waves.
- Growing needs periodic watering, not constant attendance. Water comes
  from rain, from a being carrying it, or from a water source. A water
  source is just that — no separate river concept.
- Harvest is autonomous and costs trips. A being carries a limited amount,
  so a large harvest takes several journeys.
- Several beings can work one plot at once, sharing what remains. **How
  many pile onto one plot is a weight, not a cap** — a being decides
  between joining a crowded nearby plot and walking further to an empty
  one.
- How much is standing in a field needs to be readable at a glance.
