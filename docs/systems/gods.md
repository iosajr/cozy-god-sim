# Gods

Deferred. This records what is already decided, so it is not re-litigated
when it gets built.

## Decided

- Gods act for their own reasons, self-interested as often as cosmic, and
  react to what the world already does more than they author it.
- How openly a god acts depends on its personality. Some are boisterous.
- Gods existing is common knowledge in the world. It is not a per-being
  belief, and there is no faith stat.
- **A god can never force a being's will.** Gods cause direct physical and
  environmental effects — wreck a harvest, seed chaos — but cannot compel
  anyone to act for them. This rules out mind-control and starting a war
  by decree alike.
- A god has a domain: a kind of thing, act or place it is tied to.
- **Gods are meant to arise from the perceived world, not be authored as
  a fixed roster.** Any hand-written list is scaffolding.
- The player is not a god. They watch, and have a quiet effect at the
  margins. Any real effect on the world is meant to route through a god.

## Disasters

A storm, plague or other calamity striking a settlement. Internally it is
tagged as either nature or a deliberate act by a god — that distinction is
for bookkeeping only and is never visible in the world.

## Open

- The mechanism by which gods arise from the world.
- The system for borrowing or invoking a god's power, which is the
  intended route by which the player ever affects anything.
