# Beings & Species

Every living thing, sharing one shape.

## Decided

- A species is an authored resource, not a subclass: lifespan, speed,
  diet, gestation, size, appearance, and which behaviours it is granted.
  Adding a species is filling in fields.
- One Being class underneath: id, position, species, born at,
  personality, memories, current task.
- Subclass only for a difference in kind — something that builds versus
  something that cannot, rooted versus walking. Never for different
  numbers.
- Nothing stores age. It stores when it was born.
- **Less-central species get fewer systems, not a cheaper version of the
  same ones.** Domesticated animals skip survival needs entirely rather
  than running a reduced version. Apply this to every species that will
  exist in much larger numbers than villagers.
- Wild and predator species are the exception: they need more systems,
  not fewer. Hunting is the same underlying mechanic whether a stranded
  villager or a wolf is doing it.
- Personality is a small fixed trait vector carried by every acting
  being. It has real mechanical effect: a bold one fights, a fearful one
  flees.

## Standing

- A being a god is deliberately attending to is Favored, for good or
  ill.
- A being that has visibly risen through a god's hand is Renowned. For
  animals and plants this is a transformation toward something more
  humanoid — a horse toward a centaur, a tree toward a dryad. **Each
  such species therefore needs a second set of art**, which is a real
  content cost when scoping species.

## Reproduction

- Male and female, plus time, produces offspring. It happens
  autonomously in the background, never triggered by the player.
- A being must be at least 18 to pair.
- A starting population is seeded with a spread of ages so it is
  immediately able to pair and does not read as uniform.
- Families group beings and can carry a bias that nudges what their
  members take an interest in.

## Interest

- A weighted bias on which task a being picks, driven by traits and
  needs rather than by profession.
- Intended long-term mechanism: a being that spends time near someone
  practising an interest picks it up. This needs children to exist
  first, so there is someone to learn from.

## Open

- What age does beyond counting: life stages, death, work capacity.
- Whether a pairing ever ends or cools down. Nothing currently stops a
  standing pair producing offspring indefinitely.
